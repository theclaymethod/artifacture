## Limits

ISO uses painter ordering for separate parts, not arbitrary intersecting-solid occlusion. Auto-fit covers rest geometry; animated extremes require authored bounds. Motionmaxxing's overshooting curves and runtime are not included in the bounded sampler, and its promotional-film timing observations are not independently reproduced measurements. The circuit is a silent component demonstration. No comparative renderer benchmark, narration quality check or final artifact-review certification is claimed.
