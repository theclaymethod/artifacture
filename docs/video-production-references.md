# Video production references

These user-supplied references inform authoring choices. Repository source and observed renders supply evidence for implementation claims. A reference does not change the user's authorization or select an AI model.

## Script structure

The [technical explainer field guide](https://muse.ai/s/top-technical-explainer-videos-jxh6bxv9t04xjb) is a written analysis with linked examples. We adopt an opening that shows a change, an opportunity to predict, an observed result, one causal explanation, and a usable conclusion. Recurring subjects and questions connect episodes.

Its proposed short-form timings are planning suggestions. Reach figures and research assertions were not independently verified for this integration. The [script iteration](video-script-iteration.md) applies the structure to original narration about this repository.

## Repeatable production

Rari's [Motion Engineering article](https://x.com/0xwhrrari/status/2105643919119696297) separates creative intent, references, timing, rendering, and critique. We adopt explicit beat purpose and entry/exit states, direct time sampling, inspection of rendered transitions, local repairs, and composition changes for each aspect ratio. Its model comparisons are outside this integration.

The corresponding owners here are collection briefs, [shared themes](shared-themes.md), [scene and sequence source](graphics-and-video.md), the browser exporter, and review records. Audio needs the same authored clock when added; these initial specimens are silent. A successful export establishes a generated artifact, while script approval and delivery review remain separate observations.

## Apply the references

For each beat, identify what the viewer sees at the start, what changes, what they understand at the end, and the evidence supporting that understanding. Inspect the actual opening, reading holds, transition boundaries, final state, and a repeated sample after a backward seek. Record defects with a location and a bounded correction.

Preserve editable source, shared object identity, and the evidence record when revising. Choose format layouts for their reading conditions. Keep the [collection script and storyboard](video-collections.md) available for the next episode and the next authoring run.
