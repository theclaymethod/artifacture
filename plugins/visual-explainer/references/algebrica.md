# Algebrica

Informed by the reading surface of [Algebrica](https://github.com/antoniolupetti/algebrica), this is an independent theme adaptation; upstream mathematical content and illustrations are not bundled.

Use `preset="algebrica"` for a scholarly reading surface: warm pale blush/stone (`#f4f0ef`), charcoal (`#312f2f`), EB Garamond headings and prose, Inter for diagrams and controls, fine rules, and generous margins. See [algebrica.html](../templates/algebrica.html) for the standalone template.

Lead with a descriptive title and a useful introductory sentence. Keep serif body copy at least 20px with a comfortable reading measure; use a restrained heading scale and clear section spacing. Tables, equations, code, and diagrams sit in the reading flow and inherit the page's ink and paper.

Use notes and citations when they establish provenance or explain a qualification. Do not add issue numbers, journal labels, dates, author bylines, or technical classifications unless the source supplies them and the reader needs them. Avoid decorative formulas, giant quotation marks, and numbered section furniture.

The theme changes the presentation, not the evidence. Follow [charts.md](charts.md) for data and [diagram-design.md](diagram-design.md) for relationships. Preserve readable labels and inspect both desktop and mobile through [verification.md](verification.md).
