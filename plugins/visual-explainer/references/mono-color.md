# Mono Color

Use `preset="mono-color"` for an editorial page or poster with one or two inks on neutral paper. The direction is informed by [Monocolor Editorial Print](https://github.com/yanliudesign/mono-color-skill): asymmetrical composition, visible paper, and a narrowly assigned second ink. Upstream example images are references, not reusable template assets.

The built-in adaptation uses cobalt (`#2148b8`) with terracotta (`#c65f38`). One ink carries the main content; the second marks a meaningful comparison or emphasis. An explicit one-ink request stays one ink. Keep readable text in the strongest ink and verify contrast on the chosen paper.

Use generous margins and an offset composition when it fits the content. Keep titles direct, body copy at least 16px, and diagram labels at least 14px at displayed scale. No decorative numbering, labels, stamps, seals, or fabricated publication metadata.

For an image-led poster, choose one recognizable subject that carries the message. Use an original or licensed photograph or illustration, a decisive crop, and visible halftone within the image. Let large type and the image share the composition; keep text editable. The [repair starter](../templates/mono-color.html) demonstrates this with an original two-ink illustration.

Keep texture inside imagery, never behind text or across a diagram canvas. Nodes, axes, edge labels, and connectors stay crisp. Use [charts.md](charts.md) and [diagram-design.md](diagram-design.md) for their semantics, then complete [verification.md](verification.md).
