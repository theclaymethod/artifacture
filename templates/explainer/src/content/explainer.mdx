# Direct WGSL scenes inside technical explainers

This explainer demonstrates `@artifacture/gpu` and `@artifacture/kit` inside a standalone Vite + MDX document. Every `.wgsl` shader resolves at build time through `@vgpu/wgsl/loader-vite` while Dawn renders static PNG fallbacks for offline exports and non-WebGPU browsers.
