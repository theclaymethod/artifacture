# ISO

Default explanatory theme for architecture walkthroughs, diagrams, and technical slides.

## Palette
- bg: `#ffffff`
- surface: `#f7f8fa`
- text: `#16181d`
- muted: `#5e6573`
- rule: `#dde2ea`
- accent: `#079fba`

## Typography
- display: `'Inter', ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif`
- body: `'Inter', ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif`
- mono: `'JetBrains Mono', ui-monospace, monospace`
- Floor: 18px minimum on 1920x1080 slides (24px+ for reading labels).

## Decorator Gate
- Keep prose and diagrams on open paper.
- Reserve GPU (`vgpu`) scenes for covers, structural hinges, or spatial continuity.
- Never add kickers, decorative numbering, status pills, or redundant captions.
