# Explainer fonts

The fonts of trust-map explainer pages (`../explainer-type.mjs`). Each one is licensed under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1; its licence text is beside it. The files here are subsets: Latin, Latin-1
and the punctuation the pages use, normal style, the weights listed, made with fontTools
`pyftsubset` from the static Latin woff2 files of the Fontsource packages. The export embeds the
chosen pairing in the page as data URLs.

Source Serif 4 declares the Reserved Font Name "Source". A subset is a Modified Version under the
OFL, so the subset is renamed Reading Serif in its name table, and the page names it Reading Serif.
